Why create a website in 2026

Pros, cons, differences between Google and social media, Meta ads and why owning your base still matters.

6 June 20266 minBy Samuel, WF-Studio · Web Fuengirola

Own a stable source of business information

A website gives the business control over its offer, proof, contact paths and customer data permissions. Social profiles and marketplaces can change reach, formats or account access without notice.

Connect discovery channels to one destination

Use Google, social media, ads and AI assistants to attract attention, then send people to relevant pages that explain the service and capture measurable enquiries.

Frequently asked questions

Is a social profile enough for a small business?

It may be enough to test demand, but it is a fragile long-term base because structure, reach and access are controlled by the platform.

What is the smallest useful website?

A fast page with a precise offer, proof, service area, common questions, privacy information and a working contact route.
