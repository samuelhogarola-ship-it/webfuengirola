How much a website costs in Fuengirola and Malaga

What really affects the price, when a simple website is enough and why comparing only the final number can be misleading.

5 July 20266 minBy Samuel, WF-Studio · Web Fuengirola

Price the scope, not the number of screens

Cost depends on content preparation, languages, booking or payment integrations, SEO requirements and who maintains the site. Ask for a written scope that lists deliverables and exclusions.

Compare ownership and ongoing costs

Separate the build fee from domain, hosting, licences, maintenance and future changes. Confirm who owns the domain, source files, analytics and accounts after launch.

Frequently asked questions

Why can two similar-looking websites have different prices?

Research, copy, accessibility, performance, integrations and testing are mostly invisible in screenshots but strongly affect effort and reliability.

What should a quote include?

Pages or templates, content responsibilities, revisions, integrations, launch tasks, support period and recurring fees.
