Personal trainer website: from service to first enquiry

What to include on a personal trainer website: options, service area, process, trust and a simple first enquiry.

6 October 20266 minBy Samuel, WF-Studio · Web Fuengirola

Sell the coaching method, not generic motivation

Define who the programme suits, session format, location or online delivery, assessment process and realistic progression. Show qualifications and real client evidence with permission.

Make the first contact easy to qualify

Offer one clear consultation route and ask only about goals, availability, location and relevant constraints. Health details should move to an appropriate private process.

Frequently asked questions

Should a trainer publish package prices?

At least explain the pricing structure and what changes the cost; transparent starting points reduce unsuitable enquiries.

What content builds trust for personal training?

Specific methods, coach credentials, training environment, realistic case stories and clear safety boundaries.
