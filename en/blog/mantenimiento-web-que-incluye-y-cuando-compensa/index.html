Website maintenance: what it includes and when it pays off

Updates, security, changes, basic SEO and technical support explained for local businesses.

17 July 20266 minBy Samuel, WF-Studio · Web Fuengirola

Separate maintenance from new development

Routine maintenance covers updates, backups, security monitoring, uptime checks and small content changes. New templates, integrations or campaigns should be estimated separately.

Define response and recovery before problems

Agree who receives alerts, how quickly critical failures are handled, how backups are tested and which accounts the business owns. A backup that has never been restored is not a recovery plan.

Frequently asked questions

Does a static website need maintenance?

Yes, though usually less: hosting, forms, dependencies, certificates, analytics and content still need checks.

When is a monthly plan worthwhile?

When downtime or outdated information costs more than the plan and the business needs a named person to respond consistently.
