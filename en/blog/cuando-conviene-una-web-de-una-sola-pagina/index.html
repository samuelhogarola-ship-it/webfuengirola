When a one-page website makes sense

A one-page website can work when the goal is clear, but it is not always the best base for SEO growth.

8 de julio de 20266 minBy Samuel, WF-Studio · Web Fuengirola

Use one page for one focused journey

A single-page site works when the business has one clear audience, a compact offer and one main conversion. Visitors should understand the promise, proof, process and contact action without branching.

Know when the format becomes restrictive

Choose separate pages when services have different search intent, locations need their own evidence, or campaigns require dedicated landing pages. Navigation anchors do not replace indexable topic pages.

Frequently asked questions

Can a one-page site rank locally?

It can rank for a narrow set of searches, but it has less room to answer distinct services and locations than a structured site.

What sections are essential?

A clear hero, services, trust evidence, process, common questions and a visible contact option.
