Frequently asked questions

Your questions answered

Are prices charged in Mexican pesos?

Published MXN amounts are reference prices. The final proposal is recalculated with the current ECB exchange rate and a 5% conversion allowance.

How do you work with companies in Mexico?

We deliver remotely from Europe through scheduled meetings, email or WhatsApp communication, and reviewable milestones throughout the project.

Does the price include tax, domain, and hosting?

Taxes and third-party services depend on the project and are itemised in the proposal. Nothing is added without being explained before work begins.
